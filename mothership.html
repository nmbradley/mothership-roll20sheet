Ship Operations & Maintenance

Rolls 1d5+1 to determine the number of starting issues.

Rolls a Systems Check. Failure rolls once on the Maintenance Issues Table (everyone gains 1 Stress). Critical Failure rolls twice on the table (everyone makes a Panic Check).

Rolls a Systems Check after the ship takes MDMG in a confrontation. Failure rolls once on the Maintenance Issues Table. Critical Failure rolls twice on the table.

Rolls 1d100 under your Bankruptcy Save (defaults to 2d10+10) and resolves consequences from the Bankruptcy Table.

Rolls 1d10. A result under the ship's current MDMG breaks the enemy's morale -- they signal for a ceasefire.
